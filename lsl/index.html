About LSL School

A young school with an old-fashioned belief: every child can be excellent.

For two years we have been running a rigorous programme that prepares students for a world that keeps changing — with teachers who know every student by name.

Our mission

A nurturing environment that empowers students to reach their full potential academically, socially and emotionally — with critical thinking, creativity and the habits of lifelong learning.

Our goal

To be recognized as a leading school that inspires confident, compassionate and capable leaders who contribute to their community and the world.

01

Excellence

We strive for academic excellence and personal growth in every student.

02

Innovation

Modern teaching methods and technology, used where they actually help learning.

03

Compassion

A caring environment where every student feels valued and supported.

04

Integrity

Character and ethical values that last a lifetime.

Admissions

Four steps from first call to first day.

Join our family and discover how LSL School can help your child reach their full potential.

Step 1

Application

Submit the application form with the required documents.

Step 2

Assessment

Your child completes an academic assessment and a short interview.

Step 3

Review

The admissions committee reviews the application and meets the parents.

Step 4

Decision

You receive the admission decision and enrollment details.

Required documents

  • Birth certificate
  • Previous school records
  • Immunization records
  • Parent / guardian ID
  • Application form
  • Recommendation letters

Admission criteria

  • Academic performance
  • Behavioral record
  • Parent interview
  • Student assessment
  • Space availability
